Privacy Policy

Klipped is a personal app that runs on its owner's own computer. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's videos and statistics.

All data stays on that computer. Klipped has no servers, and it does not sell, share or transfer any data. Data from Google APIs is used only for these features, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
